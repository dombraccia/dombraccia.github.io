⚡ Number Flash

Offline-capable PWA for rapid-fire number comprehension
Web App / PWA

I’ve always loved learning new languages, but no matter how close to fluent I get, mastering numbers has always been a struggle. Whether listening to phone numbers or counting change, there's always a split-second delay while my brain translates the digits.

I built Number Flash to solve this exact bottleneck. It’s designed to train rapid-fire recall, pairing visual numbers with their correct native pronunciation so you can understand them instantly without translating them in your head first.

Key Features

🌍 Multiple Languages

Active support for French (🇫🇷) and Italian (🇮🇹), helping you build direct comprehension in both target languages.

📊 Smart Analytics

Tracks your response speed (last 10 flips) and accuracy to sort cards into difficulty buckets for targeted study.

🗣️ Auto Read Aloud

Integrated text-to-speech engine speaks the numbers aloud on card flip to link auditory and visual memory.

📲 Mobile-First PWA

Installable on iOS and Android home screens. Runs 100% offline with progress stored entirely on your device.
